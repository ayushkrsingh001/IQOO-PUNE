The menagerie

Five bad habits. Five beasts.

Every monster is a posture pattern in disguise. Learn their triggers — and their weaknesses.

Neck Snake

Trigger: forward head

Slides in when your head creeps toward the screen.

Counter-move: pull your head back over your shoulders.

Shoulder Wraith

Trigger: uneven shoulders

Drags one shoulder down while you're not looking.

Counter-move: relax and bring both shoulders level.

Back Stone

Trigger: torso lean

Piles weight on your back the further you hunch.

Counter-move: bring your chest back and sit upright.

Eye Ghost

Trigger: missed breaks

Haunts marathon sessions when you forget to look away.

Counter-move: stand up, look away, move for 20 seconds.

Tension Beast

Trigger: restless stillness

Feeds on repeated slip-ups and long frozen stretches.

Counter-move: shake out the tension with a movement break.

Weekly Boss

Trigger: classified

A bigger, meaner challenge is stomping its way into a future update.

Counter-move: keep your streak ready.

Questions

Asked frequently.

Everything people want to know before they let a snake judge their posture.

Is Habitus a medical device?

No. Habitus is a posture-awareness game — it doesn't diagnose, treat, or cure anything. If you have pain or a medical condition, talk to a professional. We just make sitting better weirdly fun.

Does it record or upload my camera feed?

Never. Frames are processed in memory on your device and instantly discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere — only friendly aggregates like XP and streaks are kept.

Does it work offline?

Completely. There are no accounts, no cloud, and no network calls. The whole game — monsters included — lives on your device.

What happens if I just… ignore the monster?

It grows bolder, your session score suffers, and your streak starts sweating. The monsters are annoying by design — that's the point. Sitting tall is always the faster way out.

When can I download it?

Habitus is a playable prototype right now — store listings are on the way. The download buttons above will cheerfully tell you the same thing.
